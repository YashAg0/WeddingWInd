# WeddingWithIndia — Master Pricing, Payout & Unit Economics

## Purpose
This is the proposed commercial planning model. It separates customer pricing, fixed host payouts, fixed agent payouts, PayPal costs, reserve and WWI contribution.

## Core assumptions
- Planning FX: ₹95.50/USD.
- PayPal international commercial receiving: 4.40% + fixed fee.
- USD fixed fee: $0.30.
- PayPal conversion spread when converting received balance to INR: 3.0% above base exchange rate.
- Internal reserve: 5.0%.
- No separate PayPal surcharge is added to the customer price.
- GST, income tax and direct wedding-delivery costs are NOT included in the WWI contribution calculation.

## Important: 'profit' definition

The workbook's 'WWI Contribution After Reserve' is NOT final accounting profit. It is what remains after modeled PayPal costs, fixed host payout, fixed agent payout and a 5% internal reserve. Actual profit must additionally account for applicable GST/taxes, direct wedding delivery costs, staff, support, marketing, refunds, chargebacks and other operating expenses.
